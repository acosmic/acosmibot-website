World of Warcraft Characters

Look up Retail and Classic characters in Discord with Blizzard-grounded profile cards, combat stats, gear, talents, PvP records, raids, and available Mythic+ data.

Overview

Acosmibot reads character data from Blizzard's Profile API. Use the /wow command group for a structured lookup, or mention Acosmibot and ask naturally. WoW lookups are available on every plan and have no separate premium charge; a natural-language request still uses the normal AI-chat allowance.

What a profile includes

  • A visual full-body character card when Blizzard provides its transparent character render
  • Identity, realm, region, product, guild, item level, available combat stats, and build
  • Requester-only Overview, Gear, and Talents controls that remain active for five minutes
  • A complete profile-embed fallback when full-body media or the card renderer is unavailable
  • Clear partial-result labels when a Classic endpoint omits optional sections

Commands

/wow profile <character> <realm> [version] [region]
Show the character overview. The card uses Blizzard's full-body render when available, while requester-only buttons open equipped gear and ranked talents and can restore the original card without another lookup.

Example:

/wow profile character:Biitti realm:Spineshatter version:Classic Anniversary / TBC Anniversary region:EU
/wow pvp <character> <realm> [version] [region]
Show the PvP summary and any 2v2, 3v3, or rated-battleground brackets Blizzard exposes for that character and product.
/wow pve <character> <realm> [version] [region]
Show available equipment and raid progress. Retail also requests the character's Mythic+ profile index.

Retail/Live and US are the defaults. Character and realm are required. Choose the exact version and region whenever those defaults do not identify the character.

Ask Acosmibot Naturally

Mention Acosmibot with the same identity details you would provide to the slash command. Natural-language profile requests receive the same visual card and interactive pages.

Examples:

@Acosmibot look up Biitti on Spineshatter EU Anniversary @Acosmibot show the PvP record for Acosmic on Illidan US Retail @Acosmibot what raid progress does this character have on Classic Era?

AI tool controls

Natural-language lookup uses the server-controlled WoW Characters AI tool. Managers can disable that tool independently without disabling ordinary /wow commands. The tool is foreground-only and is never called speculatively for ambient replies.

Versions and Regions

A character name and realm are not globally unique. Acosmibot searches one explicit Blizzard product namespace rather than probing every version and risking the wrong same-named character.

Product Use It For
Retail / Live Modern World of Warcraft, including available Mythic+ profile data
Classic progression The current progression Classic product
Classic Era / Hardcore Era, Hardcore, and vanilla-style profiles
Classic Anniversary / TBC Anniversary Anniversary realms and their Burning Crusade progression

Supported regions are US, EU, Korea, and Taiwan. If “Classic” could refer to more than one product, Acosmibot asks which one you mean.

Available Blizzard Data

The character summary is required. Depending on the product and profile, Blizzard may also return:

  • race, class, faction, level, guild, achievement points, specialization, and item level;
  • health, power, armor, primary attributes, and physical, ranged, or spell statistics;
  • equipped items and specialization or Classic talent-tree data;
  • avatar and transparent full-body character media;
  • PvP summary and rated brackets, raid progress, and Retail Mythic+ profile data.

Classic profiles can be partial

Retail generally exposes the broadest profile. Classic products may omit equipment, statistics, talent details, rated brackets, raids, or full-body media. Acosmibot disables unavailable pages and labels the result as partial instead of inventing missing values.

Refresh Timing and Troubleshooting

Q: Why does my profile look out of date?

A: Blizzard profile data can lag until the character logs out. Acosmibot can only show what Blizzard currently returns.

Q: Why was my character not found?

A: Verify the character name, realm, region, and exact game product. The same name and realm can resolve differently across Blizzard's product namespaces.

Q: Why is a page or statistic missing?

A: Blizzard may not expose that optional section for the selected product or character yet. Missing data is disclosed rather than estimated.
