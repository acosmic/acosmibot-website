Reactions given and received

Reactions counts what someone adds to messages. Reactions received counts what other non-bot members add to that person's messages; self-reactions do not count. The underlying received-side metric is named reactions_received.

Received-reaction history starts August 9, 2026

Tracking is forward-only and older reactions were not backfilled. Discord raw reaction-clear events do not always identify every affected user, so cleared historical totals and the displayed top received emoji can occasionally lag.

Most Loved leaderboard

Ask Acosmibot who is “most loved,” “most liked,” or “most popular” in the server. Its live-stat tool uses the most_loved ranking based on reactions received from other people, not reactions they handed out. If no received-reaction data exists yet, the bot reports an empty ranking instead of substituting another metric.

Member Server Hub

Sign in and open /server/:guildId to see your own profile for a shared server, including the server-specific statistics and activity available to you. This member view is separate from server-management pages, which require owner or administrator access, and from Acosmibot's owner-only global admin panel.

Access and privacy

You must be signed in and share the server. The API verifies membership before returning guild-scoped data, and private AI allowance details remain visible only to the requesting member through /ai status.
