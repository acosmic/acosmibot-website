Economy & Banking System

A fully-featured currency system with Acosmicoins, daily rewards, transfers, and a global bank for your community.

Overview

Acosmibot's economy system provides a virtual currency called "Acosmicoins" that members can earn, spend, and transfer. The system integrates with games, rewards, and other features to create an engaging server economy.

Key Features

  • Acosmicoin wallet per server — earn by playing games and receiving transfers
  • Global bank for safe cross-server storage with deposit and withdrawal history
  • Give Acosmicoins to other members with /give
  • Server guild bank vault viewable with /guildbank — including current heist availability — and robbable with /heist start
  • Donate to the vault with /donate to fund events and bribe your heist Heat down; the daily Heat-relief cap resets at midnight UTC
  • Claim opt-in Good Deeds mystery drops for 500–50,000 Acosmicoins and up to 2–20 Heat relief without a wager
  • Integration with all gambling games (slots, mines, blackjack, coinflip, deathroll)
  • Leaderboards for Currency, XP, and Level via /leaderboard

The vault receives the full amount of every /donate and 10% of Acosmicoins lost in Slots, Coinflip, Blackjack, Keno, and Mines. It pays out through completed heists and the lottery. /guildbank includes recent session activity that has not reached the periodic database flush yet, so its displayed total is live.

Good Deeds

Good Deeds are opt-in, commandless mystery drops configured by server admins. The first member to claim receives a 30-second public challenge. Success awards the pre-rolled Acosmicoins and reduces Heat; failure has no penalty and reopens the generic mystery for another member.

Rewards Stay Hidden Until Success

The unclaimed card shows only the fixed ranges — 500–50,000 Acosmicoins and 2–20 Heat relief. Exact rewards are revealed only after completion, and Good Deed Heat relief does not consume the /donate daily allowance.

How It Works

💰 Acosmicoins

Acosmicoins are the virtual currency in Acosmibot. Members start with 0 Acosmicoins and can earn them through:

  • Gambling Games: Win Acosmicoins by playing slots, mines, blackjack, coinflip, deathroll, etc.
  • Transfers: Receive Acosmicoins from other members via /give
  • Lottery Winnings: Win Acosmicoins from server-run lottery events

🏦 Global Bank

The bank allows members to store Acosmicoins safely. Banked Acosmicoins cannot be lost in gambling games until withdrawn. This encourages members to save their earnings.

  • Deposits: Store Acosmicoins in the bank
  • Withdrawals: Take Acosmicoins out for spending
  • No Interest: Bank balance doesn't grow automatically
  • Safe Storage: Banked Acosmicoins are protected

Heist Minigames

After a /heist start crew forms, every member receives one random interactive job. The briefing reveals each assignment, then the shared heist message runs the jobs one turn at a time in random order. Only the named member can use the controls for their turn.

Each turn has a server-configured timer. Passing a job raises the crew's final success chance; failing or timing out lowers it. A job result only changes the final odds: one passed job does not guarantee a successful heist, and one failed job does not end it immediately.

🔓 Lockpick
Press the displayed three-number tumbler sequence in exact order using buttons 1–5. One wrong tumbler jams the lock and fails the job.
💻 Hack the Mainframe
Memorize the six-digit access code, press Enter Code, then type it into the terminal after the shared message hides it.
✂️ Cut the Wire
Compare all clues about color temperature and circuit number, then select the only wire that matches every clue.
🥸 Lookout
Match the target's hat and glasses to one of four suspects. Identify at least two targets correctly across three rounds.
🎒 Pack the Loot
Toggle the five bags until their combined weight exactly matches the getaway limit, then press Load the Van. Every puzzle has a two- or three-bag solution.
🌡️ Thermal Drill
Use Vent (-12°), Coast (+2°), Drill (+9°), and Boost (+16°) to reach the 72–82° safe band and stay there for two consecutive moves. You have four moves, and going above 95° fails immediately.

Briefing and Timeouts

Ready Up only lets the crew start before the briefing countdown ends; missing it does not fail a job. During the active phase, a turn that runs out of time counts as failed and the heist continues to the next crew member.

Commands

/balance [member]
Check your current Acosmicoin balance and bank balance. Optionally view another member's balance.

Examples:

/balance /balance @username
/give <member> <amount>
Send Acosmicoins from your wallet to another member. The amount is deducted from your server balance.

Example:

/give @username 500
/bank deposit <amount>
Deposit Acosmicoins from your server wallet into the global bank for safe keeping. Use all to deposit everything. A small fee may apply depending on server settings.

Examples:

/bank deposit 1000 /bank deposit all
/bank withdraw <amount>
Withdraw Acosmicoins from the global bank back to your server wallet. Use all to withdraw everything. Any withdrawal fee is deducted from the amount you enter.

Examples:

/bank withdraw 500 /bank withdraw all
/bank balance
View your global bank balance, total deposited, total withdrawn, and account statistics.

Example:

/bank balance
/bank history
View your recent bank transaction history, including deposits, withdrawals, fees, and dates.

Example:

/bank history
/guildbank
Check the current Acosmicoin balance of this server's shared guild vault. The guild vault funds server-wide events like the lottery, and this command shows whether a heist is available, active, or cooling down with a live timer.

Example:

/guildbank
/heist start
Rally a crew via a join button, complete turn-based crew jobs, then attempt to rob the server vault using the final odds. More crew members increase the possible total take and provide more jobs that can improve the odds; botched jobs lower them. Only one heist can run at a time, and a completed heist starts a per-server cooldown for everyone. Heists also require a minimum vault balance. Only the guild vault is at stake — personal wallets and the global bank are never touched.

Example:

/heist start
/heat
Check your Heat — your heist wanted level on this server. Heat rises when you take part in heists (more when you get caught) and lowers your crew's success odds. It cools off over time, or can be bribed down with /donate.

Example:

/heat
/donate <amount>
Donate Acosmicoins from your wallet to the server vault. Donations fund server events and heist pots, and bribe your Heat down (up to a personal daily limit that resets at midnight UTC). Donations beyond that cap still go to the vault.

Example:

/donate 5000
/leaderboard <stat>
View the top 10 members for a chosen stat. Available stats: Currency, Exp, Level, Global Currency, Global Exp, Global Level.

Examples:

/leaderboard Currency /leaderboard Global Currency

Bank Transaction History

All bank transactions are tracked and viewable with /bank history, including:

  • Deposits and the fee charged (if any)
  • Withdrawals and timestamps
  • Running balance after each transaction

📊 Viewing Your History

Use /bank history in Discord to see your recent transactions. Your user dashboard at /dashboard also shows balance history over time.

Best Practices

💡 Tips for Server Owners

  • Run lotteries via /admin-start-lotto to recirculate guild vault Acosmicoins back into the economy
  • Monitor the /leaderboard Currency to keep an eye on balance distribution
  • Set gambling limits in the dashboard to prevent runaway losses
  • Encourage banking — banked Acosmicoins can't be lost in gambling games
  • Use giveaways to reward active members with Acosmicoins

⚠️ Economy Management

  • Don't over-inflate the economy with excessive admin grants
  • Balance game payouts to maintain Acosmicoin value
  • Consider periodic "wealth taxes" or Acosmicoin sinks for balance
  • Monitor for exploitation or abuse of transfer/gambling systems

Troubleshooting

Q: Can I transfer Acosmicoins between servers?

A: Server wallet Acosmicoins are server-specific. However, the global bank is shared across all servers — you can deposit in one server and withdraw in another.

Q: What happens if I go into negative Acosmicoins?

A: You cannot go negative. Gambling games and /give will block the action if you don't have enough Acosmicoins.

Q: My deposit failed

A: Make sure the bank system is enabled on this server (check with an admin). There may also be a minimum deposit amount configured. The error message will tell you the exact requirement.

Q: Where does the guild vault come from?

A: The guild vault accumulates Acosmicoins from server economy activity. Admins can run lotteries that pay out from the vault to reward community members.
