Heist, Heat & Donations

Rally a crew, complete interactive jobs, and rob your server's shared vault while managing the Heat you attract.

Overview

Heist is a cooperative game where members team up to rob the server vault. The crew completes one interactive job per member, and every result changes the odds of the final attempt.

Key Details

  • Run /heist start to open a crew lobby with a join button
  • Every crew member receives one randomly assigned interactive job
  • Passed jobs raise the final success chance; failed or timed-out jobs lower it
  • More crew members increase the possible total take and add more chances to improve the odds
  • Crew Heat applies a separate penalty to the final success chance
  • Only the shared server vault is at stake — personal wallets and the global bank are never taken

Commands

Rally a crew through a join button. When the lobby closes, the crew completes turn-based jobs before the bot rolls the final vault attempt.

How a Heist Works

1

Build the crew

Members join through the lobby button. Only one heist lobby or active run can exist in a server at a time.

2

Review the assignments

Every crew member receives one random job. Jobs are dealt without repeats until all nine job types have been used.

3

Ready up

The active phase begins when everyone presses Ready Up or when the briefing timer ends. Missing Ready Up does not fail your job.

4

Complete each turn

Jobs run one at a time in random order. Everyone can watch, but only the member named for the current turn can use its controls.

5

Roll the final attempt

Passed jobs raise the crew's odds, failed or timed-out jobs lower them, and crew Heat applies an additional penalty before the final roll.

Jobs Change the Odds

Passing a job does not guarantee the heist succeeds, and failing one does not immediately end the run. Each result changes the crew's final success chance.

Minigame Rules

🔐 Lockpick
Memorize the displayed three-number tumbler sequence, then press the buttons 1 through 5 in that exact order. One wrong tumbler immediately jams the lock.
💻 Hack the Mainframe
Memorize the six-digit access code, press Enter Code, and type the same digits into the terminal modal before time expires. Opening the terminal hides the code from the shared message.
✂️ Cut the Wire
Read every clue about color temperature, odd or even circuit number, and the number range. Select the one wire that matches every clue.
👀 Lookout
Match the target's hat color and glasses style to one of four suspects. Identify at least two of the three targets correctly to pass.
🎒 Pack the Loot
Toggle bags until their combined weight exactly matches the getaway limit, then press Load the Van. Every puzzle has a solution using two or three bags.
🌡️ Thermal Drill
Use Vent (-12°), Coast (+2°), Drill (+9°), or Boost (+16°) to reach the 72–82° safe band and stay there for two consecutive moves. You have four moves; going above 95° fails immediately.
🚨 Guard Patrol
Predict the next checkpoint in three generated patrol sequences. Call at least two routes correctly to pass.
🔴 Laser Grid
Navigate four generated gates using the Left, Center, and Right lanes. Every route is solvable, but you may move only one lane per gate and touching a blocked lane fails the job.
🔢 Safe Dial
Compare exact-position and misplaced-digit feedback with four candidate codes, then choose the only code consistent with both diagnostics.

Turns, Timeouts & Ready Up

Only the Active Member Can Play

Another crew member cannot take your turn. Interactions from anyone except the active member are rejected.

Timeouts Count as Failed Jobs

Each turn has a server-configured time limit. If you do nothing, your job fails and the heist continues with the next crew member.

Ready Up Is Optional

Ready Up only starts the job phase early when the entire crew is ready. The phase begins automatically when the briefing ends, so missing it does not fail your assignment.

Availability & Cooldowns

  • The server vault must hold the configured minimum balance before a heist can begin
  • Only one heist lobby or run can be active in a server at once
  • After a real heist finishes, the configured cooldown applies to the entire server
  • No member can start another heist in that server until the cooldown ends
  • Use /guildbank to see the vault balance and current availability

Heat & Donations

Each member has their own Heat and daily Heat-relief allowance in each server. The crew's average Heat lowers the final heist chance, so laying low or donating can matter before a large job.

Daily Heat Relief

  • /donate always adds the full donation to the server vault
  • Only the Heat relief is capped; donations continue working after you reach that cap
  • The cap resets at 00:00 UTC, based on the calendar day rather than a rolling 24-hour timer

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are the minigames separate commands?

A: No. They are the nine cooperative jobs inside /heist start and do not require a separate wager or command.

Q: Can another member take my turn?

A: No. Only the member assigned to the active turn can use its controls.

Q: Does one failed job end the heist?

A: No. A failed job lowers the final odds, then play continues with the next crew member.

Q: Can a heist take credits from my wallet or global bank?

A: No. The shared server vault is the only target.
