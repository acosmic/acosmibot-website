League of Legends Public Lookups

Read public League profile, recent-match, scoreboard, player-detail, and on-demand timeline facts in an Acosmibot field report—without account linking, hidden ratings, or live-game scouting.

Availability and Scope

League lookups are available only in qualified, configured deployments. This repository's private, fixture-driven implementation is not enabled in public Acosmibot deployments until Riot eligibility, production-key, terms, privacy, support, and required notice requirements are complete. This page documents the feature behavior; it does not imply Riot endorsement.

Public data only

When configured, the feature reads public profile and completed-match data. It does not link Riot accounts, store Riot IDs or match history, expose live spectator data, reveal hidden MMR, estimate LP change, or create subjective player labels.

Commands

Every lookup requires an explicit platform. The platform is where the account plays, not the requester’s physical location.

/lol profile riot_id:<gameName#tagLine> platform:<NA1|EUW1|...>
Shows the public Riot ID, platform, level, Solo/Duo and Flex entries, and up to three champion-mastery records. Unranked and unavailable sections are labeled directly.
/lol history riot_id:<gameName#tagLine> platform:<...> [queue] [count:1..10]
Shows up to five recent eligible matches by default and ten at most, with result, time, duration, champion, role, K/D/A, CS, and derived CS per minute where valid.
/lol match riot_id:<gameName#tagLine> platform:<...> [game_number:1..10]
Shows one completed-match scoreboard. Requester-only controls can switch to player detail or explicitly load the optional timeline.

What the Cards Show

Field reportFactual coverage
ProfileRiot ID, platform, level, separate rank queues, and mastery. Missing optional data remains explicitly unavailable.
HistoryEligible public completed matches only. Custom and private games are excluded.
ScoreboardQueue, duration, patch, safe match suffix, two team totals, objectives, and up to ten participant rows.
Player detailRaw K/D/A, CS, gold, damage, vision, items, spells, and explicitly marked derived metrics such as kill participation.
TimelineLazy-loaded deterministic event summaries and minute-frame team gold state. If unavailable, the scoreboard remains valid and the absence is stated.

Icons support the data but never replace it: results are written as Victory, Defeat, Remake, or unknown; roles, champions, items, and numbers stay readable when a static asset is unavailable.

Direct Facts and Derived Metrics

Match and profile fields returned by Riot are presented as direct facts. KDA ratio, CS per minute, gold per minute, kill participation, team shares, team K/D/A and gold totals, event sequence, and gold-state summaries are calculated from those facts and labeled derived.

What Acosmibot does not infer

A card will not invent MMR/ELO, per-match LP, win probability, a performance rating, a replay, a Riot-written recap, or labels such as MVP, carried, or inting. An unavailable value remains unavailable.

AI Tool Control

In a qualified, configured deployment, managers can independently enable or disable League of Legends Lookups in the AI Tools section of the Acosmibot dashboard. The setting filters the native lol_lookup tool before model planning and is checked again before execution; it does not disable ordinary slash commands.

The tool is foreground-only, read-only, and free. A natural-language request uses the usual AI-chat allowance. It only performs a timeline fetch when the request explicitly asks for a match timeline; it is never called for ambient replies and returns no trusted URLs or raw account identifiers.

Refresh Timing and Troubleshooting

Q: Why is a match missing?

A: Match history may be empty, delayed, partially unavailable, or filtered because custom/private queues are out of scope. Acosmibot reports the returned count rather than estimating missing rows.

Q: Why is an icon missing?

A: The renderer uses allowlisted official Data Dragon assets with strict size, timeout, and request limits. An asset outage falls back to factual text and does not change the match data.

Q: Why is the Timeline unavailable?

A: Timeline data is intentionally loaded only after an explicit request. A rate limit or upstream failure disables that view for the current result while keeping the completed-match scoreboard available.
