AI Credits

Fund paid AI overage without changing your subscription tier. AI Credits are a separate wallet with explicit rates, spending controls, and member contribution options.

What AI Credits do

Your subscription includes a monthly AI allowance. AI Credits can fund eligible requests after that allowance is unavailable; they do not unlock subscription features or raise the plan's basic daily limit.

How a request is funded

  1. The included monthly allowance is used first when the operation is eligible.
  2. If paid overage is enabled, Acosmibot checks the server wallet.
  3. Personal guild fallback is considered only when the server allows it and the member has enabled both global guild spending and that guild.
  4. The full maximum charge is reserved before the request starts. Any unused reservation is released after the final charge is calculated.

Images require confirmation

/ai image shows a confirmation before a paid 350-credit charge. Canceling or letting the confirmation expire spends nothing.

Wallet controls and contributions

Server managers choose which paid operations are allowed, whether members may contribute, whether personal fallback is allowed, and the server's spending guardrails. Members control their own global guild-spending switch, per-server opt-in, and personal caps from AI Credits.

When contributions are enabled, members can add Fuel Cells to the server wallet. Contributions belong to that server wallet and cannot be withdrawn or transferred back to the member.

Common funding problems

Why did the request stop instead of spending credits?

Paid overage, that operation, or the needed funding source may be disabled, or the wallet may not have enough credits for the full reservation.

Why was my personal balance skipped?

Personal fallback needs server permission, your global guild-spending switch, and your opt-in for that server. Your caps must also allow the reservation.

Where can I see usage?

Use /ai status for private allowance and renewal details. Server managers can review the shared wallet and ledger in the dashboard; members can review personal balances and controls at /credits.
