AI Credits

Keep AI going after the included allowance runs out, without changing your subscription tier. AI Credits are a separate prepaid balance with clear rates, simple on/off controls, and member contribution options.

What AI Credits do

AI Credits are prepaid service capacity for eligible, explicit requests after included plan allowance runs out. They are separate from Acosmicoins, Acosmibot's virtual community currency, and from Free, Plus, Pro, or Max subscriptions. AI Credits extend eligible usage; they do not unlock Pro or Max tools, memory, personalities, ambient replies, or other plan features.

How a request is paid for

  1. The server's included AI allowance is used first.
  2. When that runs out, the server's credits are used.
  3. When the server has no credits left, your own credits are used, but only if you switched on Use my credits in <server> for that server at /credits. Each server starts off.
  4. The most a request can cost is set aside before it starts. You are charged only after the reply is delivered, and anything not used goes straight back to the balance.

Nothing is spent until someone buys credits. A server with no credits and members who have not switched on their own credits simply stop at the included allowance.

A request is paid from one balance and is never split. Server admins can limit spending by role, channel, AI feature, per-request maximum, and daily caps, and your own daily and monthly caps always apply. Background and automatic replies never spend AI Credits.

Server settings

For a server, using the server's credits and letting members use their own credits are both on unless an admin turns them off in the server Billing page. Every AI feature (chat, tool chat, images, vision) can use credits unless an admin switches it off there.

Direct messages

When Acosmibot turns on credits in direct messages, text chat with Acosmibot in a Discord DM uses your own credits. There is no setting for you to change. Credits in DMs may not be turned on yet; the /credits page shows whether they are available right now.

Images require confirmation

/ai image and mention-based image requests show an editable confirmation before included quota or a paid charge of 350 AI Credits. Canceling or letting the five-minute confirmation expire spends nothing. Confirming rechecks the live allowance, server settings, and balance; if the included unit disappeared while the confirmation was open, Acosmibot asks for a fresh confirmation instead of charging silently.

Wallet controls and contributions

Use private /ai credits to check your balance and whether your own credits can be used in the current server. Open AI Credits to buy Fuel Cells for yourself, set your daily and monthly caps and low-balance alerts, and switch Use my credits in <server> on or off for each server. Server owners and administrators manage the server's credits, which AI features they pay for, role and channel access, whether members may use their own credits there, contributions, caps, and alerts from the server Billing page.

Any current server member can open the authenticated member hub at /server/<server-id>. When contributions and sales are enabled, a member can buy a Fuel Cell directly for the server wallet and choose a named or anonymous contribution. The optional Server Boost Log shows the display choice, Fuel Cell, amount, status, and time—never personal balances, prompts, individual consumption, payment identifiers, or DM activity. Refunds and disputes remain visible as reversed entries instead of disappearing.

Allowance renewal and status

AI Credits do not renew with included allowance. Free monthly allowance resets with the UTC calendar month; paid allowance follows the server's subscription anniversary window.

/ai status posts the server's renewal time, included usage, paid-image counts by funding source, and shared server balance without exposing a member's personal wallet. Use the private /ai credits response for personal balance and controls.

Common funding problems

Why did the request stop instead of spending AI Credits?

Usually nobody has bought credits yet, or the balance is too low for the most the request could cost. A request can also stop if Acosmibot has paused spending, an admin switched off the server's credits or that AI feature, a role or channel limit applies, or a cap was reached.

Why weren't my own credits used?

Your credits are used in a server only after you switch on Use my credits in <server> for it at /credits, and only if that server's admins haven't turned off members using their own credits. Your daily and monthly caps must also leave room for the request.

Where can I see usage?

Use public /ai status for server allowance and renewal details. Server managers can review the shared wallet and ledger in Billing; members can privately review personal balances and controls with /ai credits or at /credits.
