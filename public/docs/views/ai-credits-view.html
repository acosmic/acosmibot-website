AI Credits

Fund paid AI overage without changing your subscription tier. AI Credits are a separate wallet with explicit rates, spending controls, and member contribution options.

What AI Credits do

AI Credits are prepaid service capacity for eligible, explicit requests after included plan allowance runs out. They are separate from Acosmicoins, Acosmibot's virtual community currency, and from Free, Plus, Pro, or Max subscriptions. AI Credits extend eligible usage; they do not unlock Pro or Max tools, memory, personalities, ambient replies, or other plan features.

How a request is funded

  1. The included monthly allowance is used first when the operation is eligible.
  2. If paid overage is enabled, Acosmibot checks the server wallet.
  3. Personal guild fallback is considered only when the server allows it and the member has enabled both global guild spending and that guild.
  4. The full maximum charge is reserved before the request starts. The actual charge settles only after screened content is delivered, and any unused reservation is released.

A request uses one complete wallet and is never split. Role, channel, operation, per-request, and daily caps can still block spending. Ambient and autonomous replies never spend AI Credits.

Images require confirmation

/ai image and mention-based image requests show an editable confirmation before included quota or a paid charge of 350 AI Credits. Canceling or letting the five-minute confirmation expire spends nothing. Confirming rechecks the live allowance, policy, and balance; if the included unit disappeared while the confirmation was open, Acosmibot asks for a fresh confirmation instead of charging silently.

Wallet controls and contributions

Use private /ai credits for personal balance, reserved amount, mode, DM spending, guild fallback, current-server consent, and chat scope. Open AI Credits to buy personal Fuel Cells and manage caps, low-balance notifications, DM spending, and per-server consent. Server owners and administrators manage the shared wallet, eligible operations, role and channel access, personal fallback, contributions, caps, and notifications from the server Billing page.

Any current server member can open the authenticated member hub at /server/<server-id>. When contributions and sales are enabled, a member can buy a Fuel Cell directly for the server wallet and choose a named or anonymous contribution. The optional Server Boost Log shows the display choice, Fuel Cell, amount, status, and time—never personal balances, prompts, individual consumption, payment identifiers, or DM activity. Refunds and disputes remain visible as reversed entries instead of disappearing.

Allowance renewal and status

AI Credits do not renew with included allowance. Free monthly allowance resets with the UTC calendar month; paid allowance follows the server's subscription anniversary window.

/ai status posts the server's renewal time, included usage, paid-image counts by funding source, and shared server balance without exposing a member's personal wallet. Use the private /ai credits response for personal balance and controls.

Common funding problems

Why did the request stop instead of spending AI Credits?

A platform spending pause, disabled server pool, disallowed operation, insufficient full-wallet balance, role or channel restriction, personal cap, disabled global guild fallback, or missing current-server consent can all block a paid request.

Why was my personal balance skipped?

Personal fallback needs all three keys: server permission, your global guild-spending switch, and your opt-in for that server. Your caps and the server's live operation policy must also allow the full reservation.

Where can I see usage?

Use public /ai status for server allowance and renewal details. Server managers can review the shared wallet and ledger in Billing; members can privately review personal balances and controls with /ai credits or at /credits.
