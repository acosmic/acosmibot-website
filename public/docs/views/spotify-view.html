Spotify

Turn your server into a music leaderboard. Acosmibot records what members listen to and powers .fmbot-style WhoKnows leaderboards, now-playing cards, and personal top charts — no Spotify account required to take part.

Overview

There are two ways your listening is tracked, and you can use either or both:

Two tracking modes

  • Presence (no login): When a server enables tracking, anyone showing "Listening to Spotify" on Discord has their plays recorded automatically.
  • Linked account (optional): Connect your Spotify account to also capture plays from mobile and other devices — even when Discord isn't showing them — and unlock richer commands like top artists, off-Discord now-playing, and playback control.

Connecting your Spotify account

Linking is optional — you'll appear on WhoKnows leaderboards from Discord presence either way. Link only if you want the extra features. There are two ways to connect:

On the website
Go to acosmibot.com/settings → Connected Accounts → Link. You'll be sent to Spotify to approve access, then returned to your settings.
/spotify link
Run /spotify link in Discord and click the private link it sends you. It goes through the same Spotify approval screen.

To disconnect, use Unlink on the settings page or /spotify unlink. Your stored tokens are encrypted at rest and are removed immediately when you unlink.

Commands

/spotify now [user]
Show what you (or another member) are playing right now.
/spotify whoknows [query] [type]
See who in the server listens to an artist, track, or album. Defaults to what you're currently playing.
/spotify top [type] [period] [user]
Your most-played artists, tracks, or albums. Linked users can pick a Spotify time range.
/spotify recent [user]
A member's most recent scrobbles, newest first.
/spotify taste
Summarize your listening by top genres (linked account required).
/spotify playlist
List your Spotify playlists (linked account required).
/spotify player <action>
Pause, resume, or skip playback on your active device. Requires Spotify Premium.
/spotify optout
Toggle whether your Spotify listening is tracked and shown to others.

Privacy & opting out

Tracking is off until a server enables it, and you're always in control of your own data:

Your choices

  • Opt out any time with /spotify optout, or from acosmibot.com/settings → "Keep my listening private."
  • Opting out stops new plays from being recorded and hides your listening from other members' /spotify lookups. It takes effect immediately.
  • Linked-account tokens are encrypted at rest and deleted the moment you unlink.

For server owners

Nothing is recorded until you switch it on. Open your server dashboard → Spotify (or run /spotify config enabled:true) to enable Scrobble tracking. Turning it off keeps existing history but stops recording new plays. Members can still opt out individually regardless of the server setting.
