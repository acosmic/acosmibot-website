Spotify

Turn your server into a music leaderboard. Acosmibot records what members listen to and powers .fmbot-style WhoKnows leaderboards, now-playing cards, and personal top charts — no Spotify account required to take part.

Overview

No login or account linking needed — tracking runs entirely off Discord's rich presence:

How tracking works

  • Presence (no login): When a server enables tracking, anyone showing "Listening to Spotify" on Discord has their plays recorded automatically — that's all it takes to appear on WhoKnows leaderboards and build your stats.

Commands

/spotify now [user]
Show what you (or another member) are playing right now.
/spotify whoknows [query] [type]
See who in the server listens to an artist, track, or album. Defaults to what you're currently playing.
/spotify top [type] [user]
Your most-played artists, tracks, or albums, ranked from your recorded scrobbles.
/spotify recent [user]
A member's most recent scrobbles, newest first.
/spotify optout
Toggle whether your Spotify listening is tracked and shown to others.

Privacy & opting out

Tracking is on by default, but you're always in control of your own data:

Your choices

  • Opt out any time with /spotify optout, or from acosmibot.com/settings → "Keep my listening private."
  • Opting out stops new plays from being recorded and hides your listening from other members' /spotify lookups. It takes effect immediately.

For server owners

Scrobble tracking is on by default in every server. To turn it off, open your server dashboard → Spotify (or run /spotify config enabled:false). Turning it off keeps existing history but stops recording new plays. Members can still opt out individually regardless of the server setting.
