Good Deeds Mystery Drops

Claim a commandless community challenge, solve it in public, earn Acosmicoins, and cool off your Heist Heat.

How Good Deeds Work

Good Deeds are an opt-in server feature. Admins choose one to ten text channels and a cooldown window in the website dashboard. After the previous mystery closes, Acosmibot schedules the next one randomly inside that window and posts it in one configured channel.

No Slash Command

Members join from the public Claim Good Deed button. There is no Good Deeds command, wager, leaderboard, or separate game-stat row.

Claim, Attempt, Reopen or Win

1

Find the mystery

The unclaimed card reveals only the possible Acosmicoin and Heat ranges. The challenge and exact payout remain hidden.

2

Win the claim

The first eligible member to press the button receives a fresh random challenge. Bots, non-members, and members banned from bot interactions cannot claim.

3

Play for 30 seconds

The shared message reveals the scenario. Everyone can watch, but only the claimant can use its controls.

4

Reopen or complete

A miss or timeout has no penalty and restores the generic card for someone new. A win closes the mystery and reveals the exact rewards.

Hidden Rewards

500–50,000 Acosmicoins
The Acosmicoin reward is rolled before posting: 70% from 500–5,000, 25% from 5,001–20,000, and 5% from 20,001–50,000.
2–20 Heat relief
Heat relief is rolled independently. Winners always receive every Acosmicoin even when they have less Heat than the roll or no Heat at all.

Separate from Donations

Good Deed Heat relief does not consume the personal daily Heat-relief allowance used by /donate.

Six Scenarios

💡 Repair the Streetlight
Use color, amperage, and symbol clues to choose the only matching fuse.
📦 Sort Food-Bank Crates
Toggle food boxes until their weight exactly fills the crate capacity.
🚰 Stabilize the Hydrant
Adjust water pressure and hold it inside the safe band for two cycles.
👛 Find the Wallet Owner
Combine several identity clues to find the one possible owner.
🚸 Guide School Traffic
Predict the next interval in the crossing signal sequence.
🐾 Rescue the Stray
Guide the stray through four obstacles using three lanes and one-lane moves.

Timing and Attempts

  • An unclaimed mystery closes after 15 minutes.
  • A claim started before closing receives its full 30-second attempt.
  • Each member gets one attempt per drop; a failed claimant cannot reclaim the same mystery.
  • Every new claimant receives a newly selected scenario and fresh puzzle.
  • Disabling Good Deeds cancels the active drop. Re-enabling starts a fresh randomized schedule.
  • The Games master switch pauses new scheduling without changing the saved Good Deeds toggle.
