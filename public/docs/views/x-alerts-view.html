X Post Alerts

Automatically send new posts from selected X accounts to a Discord channel. X was formerly known as Twitter.

Configure X Alerts →

What Gets Delivered

Acosmibot delivers original posts and quote posts from the accounts you track. Replies and reposts are skipped so the destination channel stays focused on content published by the account.

Short delivery window

Posts are checked on a shared schedule and may appear shortly after they are published. X Alerts is not an instant-streaming feed.

Plan Limits

X Alerts is available on paid server plans. Limits apply independently to each Discord server.

Configure X Alerts

1

Open X Post Alerts

Select an administrator-managed server, then choose X Posts under Social Alerts.

2

Add and verify accounts

Enter an X username. Acosmibot verifies the account and stores its immutable X user ID so username changes do not break tracking.

3

Choose the Discord route

Select one announcement channel and, optionally, one role to mention.

4

Enable and save

Turn on X post delivery and save. Draft changes do not start tracking until you explicitly save them.

Priority and Downgrades

The account order is the delivery priority. If a plan changes to a lower limit, Acosmibot preserves the full saved list but only the highest-priority eligible accounts remain active. Move an account up or down, pause it, or remove it, then save.

On Free, delivery pauses and the saved settings remain available if the server upgrades again.

Troubleshooting

  • Confirm X post delivery is enabled and the configuration was saved.
  • Confirm the tracked account is marked Active rather than Paused or Suspended.
  • Confirm Acosmibot can view the destination, send messages, and embed links.
  • Replies and reposts are intentionally excluded and will not trigger an alert.
