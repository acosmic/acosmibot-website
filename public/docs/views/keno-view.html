Keno

Pick your lucky numbers, watch the draw, and match your way to a jackpot.

How Keno Works

Start a game with /keno <bet> <spots>. Choose how many numbers (spots) to play, then pick them from a 40-number board using the dropdowns — or hit Quick Pick to have Acosmibot choose for you. When you press Draw, Acosmibot draws 10 numbers. The more of your picks that match the draw, the bigger the payout.

Command

/keno <bet> <spots>

Examples:

/keno 100 3 /keno 1000 5 /keno 25000 10

Rules

  • Bets must be between 100 and 25,000 credits.
  • Spot count must be between 1 and 10.
  • The board has 40 numbers and Acosmibot draws 10 of them.
  • Payouts depend on how many of your picks match the draw — the in-game paytable shows the multipliers for your spot count. Playing more spots means bigger jackpots but rarer hits, all the way up to 500,000x for a perfect 10/10.
  • You can cancel before drawing for a full refund, and if the game times out before a draw the bet is refunded too.

Stats

Keno results are included in /stats-games, including total games, wins, losses, wagered credits, net profit, and biggest win.
