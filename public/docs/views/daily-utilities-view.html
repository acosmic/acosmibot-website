Weather and Daily Discoveries

Check a five-day forecast, explore NASA’s Astronomy Picture of the Day, or meet the day’s featured bird.

Weather

Use /weather cityname:Houston, Texas for current conditions and a five-day forecast. Include a state or country when the city name is ambiguous. The optional unit setting chooses Fahrenheit or Celsius; Fahrenheit is the default.

The result includes a weather card and a unit switch that only the requester can use. If the location is not found, try its city, state, and country. A temporary weather-service problem may require retrying later.

Astronomy Picture of the Day

/apod posts the latest NASA Astronomy Picture of the Day with its title, explanation, and image credit. A video entry includes links to the video and NASA page instead of an image attachment.

Results are cached and refreshed periodically, so repeated requests can show the same entry. Availability depends on NASA’s feed and media.

Bird of the Day

/botd shows the daily bird’s common and scientific names, available information, and imagery. Everyone gets the same selection for the current UTC date; it changes with the UTC day.

When a recording is available, the bot replies to the bird post with an audio attachment. Missing imagery or audio does not mean the daily selection failed.

More Utilities

Use /define for a word definition, /avatar for a member’s avatar, or /ping to check bot latency. See the command list for more, or the reminders guide to schedule a personal reminder.
