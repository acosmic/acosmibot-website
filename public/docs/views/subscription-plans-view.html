Allowance Renewal Windows

  • Free monthly AI allowance resets at 00:00 UTC on the first day of each calendar month.
  • Paid monthly allowance follows the current Stripe subscription period.
  • Paid annual allowance still renews monthly on the subscription anniversary day; month-end anniversaries clamp for shorter months.
  • Daily chat limits reset at 00:00 UTC. /ai status shows the exact monthly renewal time.
  • Upgrades keep current usage and immediately raise the ceiling. Unused included allowance expires instead of becoming AI Credits.

How to Subscribe

You must own the server or have administrator permission for the server you want to upgrade.

1

Open Pricing and Sign In

Go to acosmibot.com/pricing and sign in with Discord.

2

Choose a Plan

Select monthly or annual billing for Plus, Pro, or Max, then choose Select Server.

3

Select the Server

Choose the server to upgrade. Only servers where you are the owner or an administrator are available.

4

Complete Checkout

Finish the secure Stripe checkout. Paid access activates for the selected server after checkout succeeds.

Checkout Availability

If a plan button says Coming Soon or checkout is paused, new subscriptions are not open at that moment. The Free plan remains available automatically.

Manage, Change, or Cancel

Sign in, select the subscribed server, and open Billing in its dashboard. From there you can:

  • Upgrade immediately after successful collection of any prorated charge.
  • Schedule a downgrade or monthly/annual cadence change for the next paid renewal.
  • Open Stripe to update the payment method or view invoices.
  • Cancel at the end of the current billing period.

The Billing page previews immediate upgrade charges and shows any pending downgrade or cadence change with its effective date and an option to keep the current plan. Resuming a scheduled cancellation restores renewal without changing the billing anchor.
