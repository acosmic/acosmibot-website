## Scope and mode

Terms of Service and Privacy Policy persistent routes (`/terms-of-service`, `/privacy-policy`), Read.

## Audience, job, and action

Members, server administrators, subscribers, and prospective users need to identify the authoritative record, understand its revision and scope, jump to a specific clause, and read the complete supplied legal text without visual distraction. They can switch between the two records at any point.

## Proof and content

The supplied legal clauses, company constants, external support link, cross-policy links, last-updated date, and real section count are the proof. No legal wording, company identity, retention period, subscription term, limitation, or disclosure is invented or omitted.

## Direction and memorable moment

Governance Constellation: a document record appears as an orbiting policy node before resolving into one opaque indexed ledger. A sticky desktop section rail and expandable mobile index make the long record navigable; sections become one continuous reading flow rather than a card stack.

## Constraints

Preserve every clause and link, semantic heading order, direct anchor navigation, shared PublicNav and SiteFooter, readable 65–75ch measure, high dark-mode contrast, keyboard focus, mobile containment, reduced motion, and route-level current-document indication.

## Unresolved decisions

The policy content remains publication-managed source text. This redesign does not add acceptance controls, version history, downloadable files, or legal advice.
