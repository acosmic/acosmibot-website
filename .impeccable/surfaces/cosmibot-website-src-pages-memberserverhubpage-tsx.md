## Scope

Authenticated member-facing server hub. Mode: Operate.

## Audience and job

Current Discord members need a safe community destination that shows their place, broad aggregate activity, the shared AI Fuel Reserve, and a clear way to contribute a server-owned Fuel Cell.

## Content and constraints

Show only membership-gated, privacy-safe aggregate activity. Never show prompts, individual AI use, personal wallets, or payment identifiers. Administrators get a Manage server exit. Contributions and the public boost log each remain independently configurable by administrators.

## Direction

Extend the Cyan Command Deck as a member station: a wide server signal header resolves into personal and shared instrument readings, then a reserve console with a community boost ledger. The memorable moment is the Fuel Reserve sitting beside visible community support, not a generic admin dashboard or card grid.
