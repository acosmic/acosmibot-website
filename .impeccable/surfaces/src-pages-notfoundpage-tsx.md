## Scope and mode

The wildcard 404 route for every unknown SPA location, Persuade through recovery.

## Audience, job, and action

Any visitor who followed a stale, mistyped, or removed URL needs immediate confirmation that the route is unknown and a confident path back to Home, Documentation, or their Servers.

## Proof and content

The actual unmatched pathname is shown as the failed coordinate. Home, Documentation, and Servers are real registered destinations; no diagnostic cause beyond “no route match” is asserted.

## Direction and memorable moment

Lost-Signal Constellation: the visitor’s route appears as a disconnected coral 404 node surrounded by three clickable known signals. The page turns an error into a miniature working route map while keeping Return home unmistakably primary.

## Constraints

Preserve the shared PublicNav and SiteFooter, native route links, visible pathname truncation, keyboard focus, clear primary recovery, responsive topology, no horizontal page scroll, and reduced-motion behavior.

## Unresolved decisions

The route does not attempt automatic search, redirection, or authentication-aware recommendations beyond the existing Servers destination.
